Memory Lane

How well do you know your streets?

Pick a place you know. Draw the streets within 500 m of it from memory, then lay the real map over your drawing. You're scored on the streets people know by name; alleys you get right are a bonus.

    How much help

    Your search goes to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, and the map of the area comes from OpenFreeMap. Your location, if you use it, is rounded to about 100 m. A saved image shows the streets around the pin, so share it only if you're happy for people to see the area.
